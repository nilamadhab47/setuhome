karmaṇi in prescribed duty
eva only / certainly
adhikāraḥ right / authority

kadācana at any time
karma-phala-hetuḥ motivated by results
akarmaṇi in inaction
format_quote Faithful Translation

“You have a right to perform your prescribed duty, but you are not entitled to the fruits of action. Never consider yourself the cause of the results of your activities, and never be attached to not doing your duty.”

Swami Gambhirananda rendition
edit_note

In Simple Words

wb_sunny

Focus entirely on giving your full presence and effort to what is in front of you right now. When you obsess over whether you will succeed, fail, or be praised, anxiety steals your joy and drains your clarity. Release the outcome to the universe—act with devotion, not calculation.

lightbulb

Today's Practice: Before starting your next task, silently dedicate the effort itself, detaching from immediate praise or validation.
