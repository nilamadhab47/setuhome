Answers & Inquiries

Frequently asked questions

When does SetuHome launch? expand_more

SetuHome opens for early members right before Diwali 2026, launching simultaneously on iOS and Android.

Is it only for people who know Sanskrit? expand_more

Not at all. Every verse comes with phonetic transliteration in the Latin script and clear, lucid English meaning so anyone can chant and understand.

Which traditions does it follow? expand_more

We present the Gita and Ramayana respectfully across multiple paramparas, noting where philosophical schools (Advaita, Vishishtadvaita, Dvaita) offer complementary perspectives.

Is the guidance AI? expand_more

Yes. Answers are synthesized from a strictly curated scripture database and always cite exact verses. It is spiritual companion contemplation and not a clinical substitute for therapy.

Is it safe for kids? expand_more

Little Setu mode features stories and audiobooks only: no open chat, no advertisements, no public commenting, and zero tracking.

Which countries are supported? expand_more

Built particularly for diaspora families in the US, UK, Canada, Australia, UAE, Singapore, and accessible wherever you build your home altar worldwide.
