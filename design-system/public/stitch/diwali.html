The Eternal Story

On this night, the citizens of Ayodhya illuminated every terrace, doorway, and earthen pathway with glowing clay lamps to welcome Lord Rama, Sita Devi, and Lakshmana back from their fourteen-year exile.

Simultaneously, we invoke Goddess Lakshmi—the sovereign deity of grace, spiritual wisdom, and righteous wealth—welcoming auspicious light into our homes to dispel inner ignorance and fear.
