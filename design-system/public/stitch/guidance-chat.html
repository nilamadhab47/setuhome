Bhagavad Gita

Chapter 6, Verse 5

Sanskrit Recitation Traditional Vedic chant · 0:38
Mūla Shloka

उद्धरेदात्मनात्मानं नात्मानमवसादयेत् ।
आत्मैव ह्यात्मनो बन्धुरात्मैव रिपुरात्मनः ॥

Transliteration

uddhared ātmanātmānaṁ nātmānam avasādayet |
ātmaiva hy ātmano bandhur ātmaiva ripur ātmanaḥ

Meaning

One must elevate, not degrade, oneself by the mind. For the mind can be the true friend of the soul, and the mind can also be its greatest enemy.

lightbulb

Sanctuary Commentary

Krishna reminds us that self-condemnation depletes the spirit. When parenting demands seem relentless, the mind easily slips into self-reproach. Lift yourself first with gentle acceptance; the loving composure you cultivate inside is the exact atmosphere your children will absorb.
